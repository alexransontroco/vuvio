## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations

### 1. **Launch Order**: España → Italia → France & Deutschland (parallel)
**Why**: España lowest regulatory risk + fastest creator onboarding

### 2. **Créateur Strategy by Tier**
- **Tier-1 (1M+)**: Direct deals, 50% Y1 commission, exclusivity
- **Tier-2 (100k-500k)**: Starter program, 45% commission, testing
- **Tier-3 (10k-100k)**: Community-led, viral growth focus

### 3. **Regulatory Approach (Minimal)**
- **ES**: AEPD notice + basic terms (€3-5k)
- **FR**: Privacy notice + GDPR basics (€10-15k)
- **DE**: Privacy policy + minimal compliance (€15-20k)
- **IT**: Standard GDPR notice (€5-8k)
