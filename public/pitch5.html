03 — POSITIONNEMENT & INNOVATION

Discovering people through what they do.

Live already exists. Vuvio does not aim to reinvent live streaming, but to organise its discovery differently. Discovery starts with an activity, a profession, a passion or an experience that interests us.

01

Activity as entry point

Discovering a creator because you are interested in what they do, without needing to know them beforehand.

02

The live stream and the POV

Seeing the gestures, the environment, the real conditions and unexpected moments from the perspective of the person doing it.

03

A geographical discovery

Exploring Lives by location and discovering activities in other cities, regions or countries.

Live streaming is not new.

The way Vuvio combines activity, point of view and geographical discovery defines its positioning.
